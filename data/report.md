## Buyer and workflow segmentation

## Opportunities to validate

1. **Evidence-linked research memos.** Hypothesis: research teams may pay to reduce document review time while preserving auditability. Test with historical filings, a fixed question set and analyst scoring; measure time saved and unsupported-claim rate.
2. **Portfolio-change explanations.** Hypothesis: middle-market wealth teams may value client-ready explanations grounded in their own approved data. Test permission isolation, reconciliation and reviewer edit time before exploring production use.
3. **Private asset document intake.** Hypothesis: extracting structured fields with confidence and document references can complement existing systems. Benchmark against manually labelled documents and explicitly route uncertain values for review.

These are product hypotheses, not demonstrated unmet demand. Established vendors may already cover some workflows. Customer interviews and competitive demonstrations are required.

## Proposed next-generation platform design

Begin with read-only document ingestion and evidence-linked answers. Keep tenant isolation, document entitlements, versioned source references and human review in the design. Separate numerical calculations from language generation and test calculations against known answers. Provide export into current reporting tools before attempting to replace the investment book of record. Autonomous trade execution is outside the proposed pilot scope.
